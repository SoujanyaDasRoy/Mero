# Mero

**A free, ad-free music and podcast player for Android.**

No account · No ads · No subscription

**[⬇ Download the latest release](../../releases/latest)**

</div>

---

Mero plays music from YouTube Music, podcasts from their own feeds, and the audio
files already on your phone — all in one place. It downloads for offline
listening, works in the car with Android Auto, and has a proper sound suite built
in. It is made for a small group of friends rather than an app store, and it
never asks anyone to sign in.

## Highlights

- 🎵 **Stream almost any song** — search as you type, and playback starts in about a second
- 🎙️ **Podcasts** — browse by category or search, and play any episode
- 📱 **Your own files** — MP3s and other audio on your phone, alongside everything else
- 🚗 **Android Auto** — browse your library on the car's screen, or just ask Google
- 🎤 **Synced lyrics** — follow along line by line, and tap a line to jump there
- 🎚️ **A real equalizer** — ten draggable bands, crossfeed and loudness matching
- 📥 **Offline downloads** — keep songs for when there's no signal
- 🎨 **Make it yours** — accent colours, dark mode, four player layouts, and your own app icon

## Features

### Listening

- Streams from YouTube Music, with lock-screen, notification, headset and Bluetooth controls
- A queue you can reorder, shuffle and repeat, with **Play next** and **Add to queue** on every song
- **A queue that learns your taste** — tap a song and what follows is music like it, chosen by what you like, play often and skip. Nothing leaves your phone
- **Keeps playing** with similar songs when the queue runs out — or pauses after an hour idle, your choice
- Playback speed from 0.75× to 2×
- **Choose where it plays** — the output button on Now Playing shows your phone, earbuds or speaker, and switches between them
- Four Now Playing layouts: **Classic, Immersive, Up Next** and **One-handed**

### Podcasts

- Search any show, or tap a category — Comedy, News, True Crime, Business, Technology and more
- Episodes list with dates and lengths; scrub freely through long episodes
- Found through the Apple Podcasts directory and played straight from each show's own public feed

### Music on your phone

- Add audio files from your phone; they appear under **On this phone** in the Library
- Open any audio file with Mero from your Files app, WhatsApp or a download notification
- Local files play directly from storage and are never copied into the cache

### Search

- Results from the first letter, across **Songs, Albums, Artists, Playlists** and **Podcasts**
- **Find a song from its lyrics** — type or say any line you remember
- **Voice search** — tap the microphone and say a song, an artist or a lyric
- **Endless results** — scroll as far as you like; when the exact matches end, songs like them keep coming
- Suggestions while you type; tap the empty search bar to see the **songs, albums and artists you opened before**, with their covers, ready to play again
- Before you type: the artists you play, what you were last listening to, podcast categories and genre tiles
- Albums, playlists and artists **open** to their track list — nothing starts playing until you choose
- A clear message and **Try again** when there's no connection

### Library and playlists

- Liked songs, recently played, most played, downloads and your phone's files
- Your own playlists, each with a **cover and description you choose** — add songs from search, from recent listening or from your phone
- **Smart playlists** that fill themselves from rules like play count, artist or recency
- **Import** a playlist from a YouTube Music link. Spotify imports work too, with your own Spotify client id and secret entered in Settings — Mero ships none, because a shared key in an app handed round a group is a key that leaks

### Home

Mero has no account, so recommendations come from the only record there is — what
you've played on this phone. Nothing leaves the device.

- **Moods** — Romance, Chill, Party, Workout, Focus, Sad, Devotional, Retro; one tap starts a mix picked to your taste
- **Made for you** — a mix for each artist you love
- **Jump back in** — what you played last
- **On repeat** — what you keep coming back to
- **More like…** — songs similar to ones you play
- **A shelf for each artist** you play most
- Shelves ordered by what you listen to, each song shown once, and your own music visible even offline
- Pull down to refresh

### Lyrics

- Synced lyrics that **follow the singer line by line**, timed to the millisecond
- **Tap any line** to jump the song to it
- Scroll ahead freely; the view returns to the current line on its own
- **Pinch** to resize the text
- Looks in several places — LRCLIB first, then YouTube Music's own lyrics — and lets you search with your own words when a match is wrong

### Sound

All processing happens inside Mero, so it sounds the same on every phone.

- **Ten-band parametric equalizer you drag** — frequency and width as well as gain, with the live spectrum behind it
- **Separate profiles** for headphones, speaker and car, switched automatically or pinned by hand
- **Presets**, including Extended and Less Bass alongside the usual genre curves
- **Crossfeed** for headphones, so hard-panned recordings don't happen inside your head
- **Loudness matching** measured the way broadcasters do it
- **Preamp with automatic headroom**, so boosting never clips
- **Beat haptics**, driven by the music itself

### Android Auto

- Browse **Recently played, Liked songs, Playlists, On this phone** and **Downloads** on the car's screen
- Search from the car, or say **"Hey Google, play … on Mero"** — voice works on the phone too
- Picking a song plays the rest of its list after it

### Gestures

| Where | Gesture | Does |
|---|---|---|
| Any song in a list | Swipe right | Play next |
| Any song in a list | Swipe left | Like / unlike |
| Any song in a list | Long-press | Song menu |
| Now Playing cover | Swipe left / right | Next / previous song |
| Now Playing cover | Double-tap | Like ❤️ |
| Now Playing | Swipe down | Close the player |
| Mini player | Swipe up / sideways | Open the player / change song |
| Lyrics | Tap a line · pinch | Jump there · resize text |
| Queue | Swipe a song away | Remove it |
| Home | Pull down | Refresh |

### Personalisation

- Twelve accent colours, or **Material You** colours from your wallpaper (Android 12+)
- System, light or dark theme, plus **pure black** for AMOLED screens
- **Six app icons** — Classic, Midnight, Paper, Coral, Ocean, Mint — or **your own photo**, framed like a profile picture, which becomes both Mero's home-screen icon and its logo inside the app
- **Song reminders** — an occasional, quiet nudge about a favourite you haven't played lately. *Now and then* by default; *Daily* or *Off* in Settings

### Reliability

- **Recovers from a dropped connection** on its own — waits for the network and carries on where it stopped
- **Carries on after other audio** — a reel, a video or a voice note in another app — once it ends
- **Remembers where you were** — reopen Mero, or press play on your earbuds, and the same song continues from the same spot
- Asks once to be **exempt from battery optimisation**, so music keeps going with the screen off
- Downloads play fully offline

### Updates

Mero isn't on the Play Store, so it checks GitHub for new versions itself, when it
opens and twice a day in the background. When one is out, a banner appears on
Home and a notification arrives once; tap **Update** to go straight to it, then
**Download** and **Install**. Your library, playlists and downloads are kept. The
download is remembered across restarts, and a file that won't install can be
discarded.

## Install

Get the APK from the **[latest release](../../releases/latest)**.

| File | For |
|---|---|
| `mero-<version>-arm64-v8a-debug.apk` | **Almost every phone — take this one** |
| `mero-<version>-armeabi-v7a-debug.apk` | Older 32-bit phones |
| `mero-<version>-x86_64-debug.apk` | Emulators |
| `mero-<version>-x86-debug.apk` | Old 32-bit emulators |

1. Open the downloaded APK on your phone.
2. Allow Android to install apps from wherever you opened it.
3. Play something.

**Requires Android 9 (API 28) or newer.** Every release is signed with the same
key, so installing over an older version keeps everything.

> **Using Android Auto?** Because Mero isn't installed from the Play Store, turn on
> **Unknown sources** once in Android Auto's developer settings, or the car won't list it.

## Privacy

- **No account and no sign-in** — ever. All requests to YouTube are anonymous.
- **Your data stays on your phone** — library, history, playlists and settings are stored locally.
- **No ads, no analytics, no tracking.**
- The photo picker gives Mero only the one picture you choose; storage permission is never requested.

## Build it yourself

```bash
git clone https://github.com/SoujanyaDasRoy/Mero.git
cd Mero
./gradlew :app:assembleDebug        # build
./gradlew :app:testDebugUnitTest    # run the tests
```

Needs the Android SDK and **JDK 17 or 21** — not 25, which the Android Gradle
Plugin does not yet accept. APKs land in `app/build/outputs/apk/debug/`, split
per architecture and named after their version.

## How it works

Two Gradle modules: `innertube/`, vendored unmodified from
[z-huang/InnerTune](https://github.com/z-huang/InnerTune), and `app/`, which holds
everything else.

- **Search and metadata** come from YouTube's InnerTube API, anonymously.
- **Stream URLs** are resolved by asking YouTube directly, with an embedded
  [yt-dlp](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp) as the fallback when that fails. The
  direct path is roughly ten times faster; the fallback is what keeps playback
  working when YouTube changes something.
- **Stream URLs are never stored.** They expire in about six hours, so Mero keeps
  only the `videoId` and resolves a fresh URL each time a track opens.
- **Two caches**, deliberately separate: temporary streaming in `cacheDir`, and
  downloads you asked for in `filesDir`, so clearing one never touches the other.
- **Playback runs in a Media3 `MediaLibraryService`**, which is what lets the
  notification, headset, Bluetooth and Android Auto all control it — and what
  recovers from dropped connections and resumes after interruptions while the
  screen is off.
- **Audio processing runs inside ExoPlayer's own pipeline** — biquad filters,
  crossfeed, loudness measurement and limiting, all Mero's own code.
- **Podcasts** use the Apple Podcasts directory to find shows and each show's RSS
  feed for episodes. **Lyrics** come from LRCLIB, with YouTube Music as a fallback.
- **No Google sign-in, ever.** Signing in would move the failure mode from "an IP
  got rate-limited" to "a friend lost their Google account". Spotify is metadata
  only — its audio is DRM-protected and Mero does not touch it; imported tracks
  are matched to YouTube.

Full rationale, including the alternatives rejected and why, is in
[`docs/architecture.md`](docs/architecture.md). Release notes for every version are
in [`docs/`](docs).

## Known limitations

- **Quality tops out at Opus ~160 kbps.** That's YouTube's ceiling without a
  signed-in Premium account, which is the one thing Mero will not do.
- **No Dolby Atmos.** It needs OEM licensing and Atmos-encoded sources; YouTube
  serves stereo.
- **Extraction breaks now and then.** YouTube changes its backend; the bundled
  yt-dlp updates itself to keep up, and occasionally a new Mero release is needed.
- **Some phones stop background playback anyway.** Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo, Realme and
  OnePlus can be aggressive about it. Mero asks to be exempt from battery
  optimisation; on these phones, also allow **Autostart** / **Background activity**
  for Mero in the phone's own settings.
- **A photo icon goes on the home screen, not in the app drawer.** Android doesn't
  let apps change their app-drawer icon to a picture, so the drawer keeps one of
  the six built-in icons. Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo and Realme phones also need **Home
  screen shortcuts** allowed for Mero; Mero says so when it's blocked.
- **A car can't be told apart from earbuds automatically** — both connect the same
  way over Bluetooth. Pin the Car profile in the equalizer instead.
- **Not every song has lyrics.** No free lyrics service covers everything; search
  with your own words when a song isn't found.

## Legal

Mero streams audio through YouTube's internal API. It is non-commercial, carries
no advertising, and is distributed privately. It does not circumvent DRM and does
not access protected audio from Spotify.

## Security

The `AIzaSy…` strings in `innertube/` are YouTube's public client keys, not
secret credentials — see [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).
