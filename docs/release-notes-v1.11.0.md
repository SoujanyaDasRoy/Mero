## What's new

### 🎙️ Podcasts
Search for any podcast in the new **Podcasts** tab, or tap a category on the Search screen — **Comedy, News, True Crime, Business, Technology** and more. Open a show, pick an episode, and it plays like any song. Scrubbing through long episodes works too.

### 📱 Music already on your phone
Add MP3s and other audio files from your phone, and they sit in your Library under **On this phone**. You can also open any audio file with Mero from your Files app or WhatsApp.

### ➕ Add songs to your playlists
Open a playlist and tap **Add songs**. Search for anything, pick from what you played recently, or add files from your phone.

### 🚗 Android Auto
Your music in the car: **Recently played, Liked songs, Playlists, On this phone and Downloads** on the car's screen. Tap the search button, or just say **"Hey Google, play Arijit Singh on Mero."** Voice requests work on the phone as well.

> Installed Mero from a file rather than the Play Store? Turn on **Unknown sources** once in Android Auto's developer settings, or the car won't list it.

### 🔊 Choose where the music plays
The first button under the controls on Now Playing shows where your music is going — *This phone*, your earbuds, a speaker — and tapping it lets you switch.

### 🎨 Your icon, your way
**Settings › App icon** has six colours: Classic, Midnight, Paper, Coral, Ocean and Mint.

Or tap **Photo** to use any picture of your own. Frame it like a profile picture, tap **Use as icon**, and it becomes **Mero's icon on your home screen** and **the logo inside the app**.

> On Xiaomi, Redmi, POCO, Oppo, Vivo and Realme phones, allow **Home screen shortcuts** for Mero first (Mero's app settings › Permissions). Mero tells you if your phone blocked it and takes you to the right screen. The app drawer always keeps one of the six built-in icons, because Android doesn't let any app put a photo there.

### 🎤 Lyrics that keep time
- **Many more songs have lyrics.** Mero now looks harder, and falls back to YouTube Music's own lyrics when it has to.
- **The highlighted line follows the singer properly.** It used to lag up to a second behind.
- **Tap any line** to jump the song there.
- Scroll ahead to read on, and tap **Back to current line** to return.
- **Pinch** to make the text bigger or smaller.
- Wrong lyrics? Tap **Wrong lyrics?** and search with your own words.

### 🔎 A better search
- **Search by lyrics.** Type a line you remember, like *"tera mera rishta hai kaisa"* or *"is this the real life"*, and the song comes up
- **Search by voice.** Tap the 🎤 in the search bar and say a song, an artist or a line of the lyrics
- **Results never run out.** Keep scrolling and more songs keep coming, and once the exact matches end, songs like them follow
- **Recent searches show the songs themselves.** Tap the search bar and the songs, albums and artists you opened before are there with their covers, one tap to play again
- **Suggestions** appear as you type, one tap to use
- **Podcast categories**, all as tidy pills
- If there's no connection, it says so plainly and offers **Try again**

### 👆 Gestures everywhere
- **On any song in any list:** swipe right to play it next, swipe left to like it, long-press for more options
- **On the album cover in Now Playing:** swipe left or right to change song, double-tap to like ❤️
- **In the queue:** swipe a song away to remove it
- **On Home:** pull down to refresh

### 🧠 A queue that knows your taste
Tap a song in search, or ask for one by voice, and what plays next is **music like that song**, not the other search results. Mero picks from YouTube's similar songs using what you like:

- **Songs you've liked** and **artists you like or play a lot** come first
- **Songs you just heard** wait a while before coming round again
- **Songs you skip** (moving on within the first 30 seconds) sink, and ones you keep skipping stop appearing
- **Variety:** never more than two songs in a row by the same artist

It all stays on your phone and gets better the more you listen. Albums and playlists still play in their own order.

### 💛 Friendly reminders
Now and then, Mero gently reminds you of a song you love that you haven't played in a while. Tap it and it plays. It's quiet (no sound, no pop-up) and only in the daytime.

Choose how often in **Settings › You › Song reminders**: *Now and then* (the default, at most every few days), *Daily*, or *Off*. If you're already listening every day, you'll never get one.

## Music that keeps playing

- **When your connection drops** — a lift, a tunnel, switching from Wi-Fi to mobile data — Mero now picks up where it stopped as soon as you're back online. Before, you had to close and reopen the app.
- **After a reel, a video or a voice note** in another app, your music carries on by itself once the other sound stops. Turn this off in **Settings › Audio** if you'd rather it didn't.
- **Battery optimisation:** the first time you play something, Mero asks Android to let it run in the background. Tap **Allow** and music keeps going with the screen off. You can check this any time in **Settings › Playback reliability**.
- **Opening Mero after closing it** now shows the song you were on, ready to carry on from the same spot.
- **Earbud and car play buttons** now resume the right song. Before, they could jump ahead to a different one.

## Smaller improvements

- **Artist pages** show the artist's songs first and open faster
- **Album covers** now show on every phone. On some phones they appeared blank.
- **Up Next** no longer lists the song that's already playing
- **Your own playlists** have the full song menu (play next, add to queue, like, share)
- **Recently played** now includes songs that played on their own, not only ones you tapped
- The play button no longer looks "playing" when playback has actually stopped
- Mero now asks for notification permission (Android 13+), so update notices can actually appear
- Several security fixes
