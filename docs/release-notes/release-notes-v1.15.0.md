## What's new in 1.15.0

### 👥 Group playlists
On any playlist, tap ⋮ → **Make it a group playlist** and send the link. Your friend taps it and chooses **Join**. From then on, **either of you can add or remove songs**, and both phones end up with the same playlist.

- Changes arrive when you open Mero, when you open the playlist, and every few hours in the background
- Add or remove songs while offline: they're sent once you're back online
- If you both change something at the same time, both changes count
- New songs go to the end. Dragging songs into your own order only changes your phone
- The name and cover are shared too
- A small 👥 icon marks group playlists. A pop-up tells you when friends added songs: "*Road trip*: 3 new songs"
- **Invite friends** sends the link again; **Leave group** keeps your songs as an ordinary playlist
- Don't want to edit together? **Just add a copy** gives you your own playlist instead

There's no account and no sign-in. The link is the key, so only send it to people who should be able to change that playlist. Group playlists are kept on Mero's share site (the name, the songs and the cover, nothing about you).

**Share playlist** still sends a copy, exactly as before.
