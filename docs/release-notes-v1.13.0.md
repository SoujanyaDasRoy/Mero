## What's new

### 🎯 Recommendations that learn your taste
What plays next is now built from **what you actually listen to**, not from song or artist names:

- Mero finds the songs that best stand for your taste: ones you've **liked** and **replayed**, weighted toward what you've been playing **lately** (old habits fade, new ones take over), with skipped songs counting against
- The queue after a song is made from **music like that song** *and* **music like your favourites**. Songs that fit both rise to the top, so it stays close to what's playing while leaning your way
- An artist's name only nudges the order slightly; it no longer decides it
- Songs you skipped, or heard just now, stay out of the way
- **Your Mix** is the first tile under Made for you on Home: a queue made purely from your taste, different every time. It improves as you listen and like more songs

It all happens on your phone: there's no account and nothing is sent anywhere.

### 🔗 Paste a YouTube link and it plays
Paste any YouTube or YouTube Music link into search (a video, a share link, a Short, or a playlist) and it shows up ready to play, even when it isn't in YouTube Music's song catalogue. Searching for the name of a live recording or fan upload now also brings up matching videos when there aren't many songs.

### 💾 Back up and restore your library
Settings → Library → **Back up your library** saves your likes, playlists, play history and settings into one file. **Restore from a backup** brings them back after a reinstall or on a new phone.

### 🟣 A new Mero logo
Mero has a new logo: a soft purple **M** on lavender. It's the app icon, the splash screen (now the logo's own gradient across the whole screen), the logo on Home, and the little icon on Mero's notifications (playback, reminders and updates). The app's default accent colour is now violet to match.

Settings → **App icon** has the logo, or **your own photo**, as before. The extra coloured icons from earlier versions are gone; if you were using one, Mero moves you to the new logo the next time you close it.

### 🫧 A floating menu bar
The bottom bar is now a floating pill, with the tab you're on in its own highlighted capsule.

### 🎚️ Better tabs for Theme and Song reminders
The Theme (System / Light / Dark) and Song reminders (Off / Now and then / Daily) choices are now a bar with an icon on every option and a highlight that slides to your pick.
