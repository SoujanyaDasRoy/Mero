**Mero 1.13.1: songs and playlists you share go out as a card, and playlists open in Mero from a link.**

> ## Which one are you on?
> - **On 1.13.0:** just install this over it. Your library, playlists and downloads stay.
> - **On 1.12.0 or older:** Android will **not** install this over it (you'll see "App not installed"), because the signing key changed in 1.13.0. Uninstall Mero first, then install this. Uninstalling clears your library; if your Google backup is on, Android may bring it back, otherwise take a screenshot of your playlists first. **This only happens once.**

## What's new in 1.13.1

### 🖼️ Share a song as a card
**Share** on a song sends a picture with the cover, the song and artist, and Mero's logo, with a link under it. On a phone that has Mero, tapping the link opens straight in Mero and plays the song, then music like it. For anyone without Mero it opens the same song in YouTube Music.

### 📤 Share a playlist as a card and a link
On a playlist, tap ⋮ → **Share playlist**. It sends a picture with the playlist's covers, its name, how many songs and Mero's logo, with one link that carries every song.

Tapping the link on a phone with Mero opens Mero and asks: **Play** it, or **Add to my library** as a new playlist (it never replaces one you have). Anyone without Mero sees the playlist's name and can play it on YouTube.

A very long playlist (over 150 songs) doesn't fit in one link: use **Share as a file**. Your friend opens the file with Mero, or goes to Settings → Library → **Import a playlist file**.

### ✨ The rest of 1.13
Everything from [1.13.0](https://github.com/SoujanyaDasRoy/Mero/releases/tag/v1.13.0) is in here too: recommendations that learn your taste, the new logo, the floating menu bar, smoother motion, and a backup that keeps itself (to a folder or to Google Drive).
