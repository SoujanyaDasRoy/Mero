**Mero 1.9.0 — albums open instead of playing, and search knows who you listen to.**

Install over 1.8.0. Your library, playlists and downloads are kept.

**If you are on 1.8.0, you do not need this page.** Open Mero, tap the banner at the top of Home, then Download and Install. This is the first release the app can deliver to you itself.

---

## Download

Take **`mero-1.9.0-arm64-v8a-debug.apk`** unless you know you need another. Requires Android 9 or newer.

---

## Albums open now

Tapping an album used to start playing it from track one. Half the reason to open an album is that you want the third song on it, so that was the one thing it should not do.

You now get the cover, the artist, the year, the track count, **Play** and **Shuffle**, and a numbered list — tap any track to start there. Playlists you find in search open the same way, and so do albums on an artist's page.

## Search suggests, instead of just sitting there

The empty search screen was a grid of coloured rectangles with a word in each. Now, above those:

- **Artists you play** — real photographs, not boxes
- **Pick up where you left off** — the songs you were last listening to

Both come from what is already on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere.

## It forgets what you searched an hour ago

The search box kept your query forever. Come back after a couple of albums and yesterday's search was still sitting there with its results, waiting to be cleared before you could start.

It now keeps it for fifteen minutes — long enough to flick to Home and back, gone by the time you have forgotten typing it.

## Gestures

- **Swipe up** on the mini player to open it
- **Swipe left or right** on the mini player to change track
- **Swipe down** on the full player to close it

## Artwork that was quietly missing

Some covers were being measured as zero pixels wide and never drawn at all — the album cover was the obvious one, but it will have affected others. Fixed everywhere at once.
