1b A · Standard
Baseline M3. Centred 330px artwork, tint pulled from the cover, transport row on the accent, secondary actions in a bottom bar. Safest, and the one that survives any artwork.
1c B · Full-bleed
Artwork runs edge to edge under the status bar and fades into the surface. Title gets display size, transport uses a squared play button, Lyrics and Queue become tonal buttons. Most expressive, most dependent on good cover art.
1d C · Queue-forward
200px artwork, compact transport, and Up next docked as a card on the same screen — queue reordering without opening a sheet. Densest, and the closest to how a shared-queue listener actually uses the app.
1e Search · typing
The focused state: suggestions collapse into live results as you type, tabbed by type, songs first. Downloaded tracks keep their marker here too, so an offline search reads the same as an online one. Recents and trending chips are in the live prototype under Search.
